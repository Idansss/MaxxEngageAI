# Maxx Engage — Verified Competence for Global Digital Talent
## A Manifesto for the Competence Engine of Civilization OS

> *"No degree? No connections? Prove what you can do."*

---

## The Problem

Humanity's bottleneck is no longer compute, energy, food, or even technology.  
It is **high-fidelity coordination among strangers who do not trust each other.**

Every macro problem — corruption, inequality of opportunity, misinformation, wasted talent — collapses to four root causes:

1. We cannot quickly identify **who actually knows what and can actually do what.**
2. We have no shared, immutable memory of **who was right in the past.**
3. Short-term incentives drown out **long-term truth.**
4. **Credentialing systems from the 19th century** still gatekeep 21st-century opportunity.

A developer in Lagos with better skills than a graduate from a European university cannot prove it.  
A writer in Nairobi, a data analyst in Accra, a designer in Kigali — invisible to the world, not because they lack ability, but because they lack the right paper from the right institution in the right country.

This is a coordination failure. Maxx Engage is the fix.

---

## The Mission

> To build a public-benefit AI system that helps humans prove competence, verify truth, learn faster, make better decisions, and coordinate action — through transparent, evidence-based, decentralized AI that no single person, company, or government can control.

**Maxx Engage** is the first engine of **Civilization OS** — a coordination substrate for human civilization.

---

## What Maxx Engage Does

Maxx Engage is a **Verified Competence Engine**. It:

1. Tests users with real-world challenges graded against transparent rubrics
2. Reviews submissions with AI — and human experts for top work
3. Issues **W3C Verifiable Credentials** the user owns, not us
4. Builds a public (user-controlled) proof of skill that travels anywhere
5. Models **skill decay** — a 2019 credential is not equal to a 2026 one
6. Resists **Sybil attacks** so credentials mean something
7. Allows **zero-knowledge proofs** — prove you're in the top 5% without revealing your score

## Principles (The Constitution)

1. **No secret scores.** Every score is visible, questionable, appealable.
2. **No raw personal data extraction.** Aggregate, anonymize, federate — or don't touch.
3. **Plurality of models.** Never one AI oracle. Always multiple, cross-checking.
4. **Open source default.** Closed-source modules require written justification.
5. **Decentralized governance from before mass adoption.** Encode it before it matters.
6. **Calibrated outputs only** for any decision affecting human opportunity.
7. **Human appeal for every AI judgment** affecting a real outcome.
8. **No advertising business model.** Grants, foundations, optional user payments.
9. **Built to outlive the founder.** Bus factor > 1 from Phase 2 onward.
10. **The system serves humans. Humans never serve the system.**

**Non-negotiable architectural principles:**
- Plurality over monoculture
- Open source from day zero (AGPL-3.0)
- User-owned data via DIDs and VCs
- Calibrated, not confident — every output ships with uncertainty
- Human-in-the-loop for any score that affects opportunity
- Antifragile — adversarial inputs make the system stronger

## The MVP (Phase 1, ~3 months)

**First niche:** African digital talent — web dev, design, data, writing, business ops.  
This is a strategic wedge: a market the West underserves, a population we understand, where the problem is most acute.

**MVP features:**
1. User signs up, picks a skill path
2. AI tests current level (adaptive, 15–30 min)
3. AI generates personalized 4–12 week learning path
4. AI issues real micro-tasks (small, gradable, real-world)
5. User submits; AI reviews against a transparent rubric
6. Top submissions enter human review queue
7. System emits a **W3C Verifiable Credential** the user owns
8. Public proof page (user controls visibility)
9. Simple appeal mechanism for any score
10. All scores, rubrics, and AI reasoning visible to the user

## What We Will Not Do

- Sell user data

- Build a black-box scoring system
- Take VC money that demands equity in governance
- Add advertising
- Ship without a human appeal path
